Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Getting Dispatched

How fast can my truck start hauling loads?

As soon as you submit your form and carrier packet (MC Certificate, W-9, Insurance), our setup team completes onboarding in less than 2 hours. Your assigned dispatcher will start searching live DAT loads immediately!

Do I keep my own MC Authority & Company Name?

YES! You maintain 100% ownership and authority. All loads are booked directly under your company name and MC/DOT number. We simply act as your dedicated back-office dispatch team.

How does the 1-click mobile approval work?

When your dispatcher finds a high-paying load, they send an interactive Load Offer directly to your smartphone or Carrier Portal. You can review the pickup/delivery cities, total miles, and gross rate, then click ACCEPT or DECLINE with 1 tap.
