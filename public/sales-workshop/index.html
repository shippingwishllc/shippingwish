How we win carriers, keep them, and start work

This book is for sales people. Read it once in the workshop. Keep it open on your desk. If you follow this, you will know what to say, where the client shows in our software, and what happens after they pay.

We are Shipping Wish LLC. We place a Dedicated Fleet Operations Manager with small U.S. motor carriers. They keep 100% of freight pay from the broker. We invoice a small weekly Stripe retainer. We are not a “dispatch company” and we do not take a percent of the load.

2. Words that kill the call

Never say
  • “We are a dispatch company”
  • “10% of the load” / commission / “we split”
  • “I have a hot load right now” on a cold call
  • “Load board” as the product (we include freight finding — we do not sell a board subscription)
  • “Guaranteed $X per mile”
  • Anything about Delaware / “US based company” as a sales pitch
Always say
  • “Dedicated Operations Manager for your trucks”
  • “Weekly Stripe invoice — small and clear”
  • “You keep 100% of freight pay”
  • “Named person, not a call center”
  • “7-day trial, $0 today”
  • “If you already have this in-house, I will hang up”

4. Your daily loop (do this in order)

  1. Login → Sales CRM & Leads.
  2. Check Website Stripe checkouts. Anyone new = call in 15 minutes. They already agreed to the trial.
  3. Open Carrier Replies. Answer every unread email.
  4. Filter leads New. Call 20. Log the call in your head: did they want a manager or not?
  5. If they said maybe: click Email (Dedicated Manager letter).

  7. SMS only if they asked for a text, or they already replied YES, or they are a paying/trial client. Cold SMS can get the company fined.

5. How to talk to a carrier

English — 20 second open

“Hi, this is [your name] with Shipping Wish LLC. I’m not calling to dump loads on you.
We place a Dedicated Operations Manager with small fleets — one named person on your trucks. Weekly Stripe invoice. You keep 100% of the freight pay.
Do you already have that person in-house, or is that still you at night?”

Roman Urdu — same meaning (practice both)

“Salam, meri naam [name] hai, Shipping Wish LLC se. Load dump nahi kar raha.
Hum chhote fleet ko Dedicated Operations Manager dete hain — ek named person aap ke trucks pe. Weekly Stripe bill. Freight ka 100% paisa aapka.
Kya yeh kaam already ghar pe koi karta hai, ya raat ko aap khud karte ho?”

If they pause

“Small weekly amount. 7-day trial, nothing due today. We find and book. You approve. Useful, or are you covered?”

If they got burned before

“Understood. This is a named manager, not a call center, and not a cut of your load. I can email a one-pager. No pressure.”

If they say yes

  1. Ask: trucks, equipment, home time, MC/DOT.
  2. Send Packet + Weekly Stripe from CRM while you are still on the phone.
  3. Stay on the line until they open the email. “$0 today. First charge after 7 days if you keep us.”
  4. Tell them an operations manager will call today or tomorrow morning.
Close line: “Link email mein hai. 7 din free. Cancel kar diya to $0. Agar rakhoge to weekly $[149/350/500]. Manager kal se trucks dekhna shuru karega.”

6. How you keep them satisfied

Sales closes. Operations keeps. But sales is responsible for a clean handoff. If you sell a dream and ops gets an empty folder, the carrier cancels in week one.

First 24 hours after Active / Stripe

  1. Call. Introduce the manager by name.
  2. Collect: MC, USDOT, COI, W-9, factoring (if any), truck/trailer, preferred lanes, who approves loads, ELD.
  3. Put files in Document Vault. Put trucks in Fleet.
  4. Send one email: “Here is your manager, here is the portal login, here is what we will send you when a load is booked.”

First 7 days (trial)

  • At least one serious load option they can say yes or no to. Never book without approval.
  • If freight is ugly that week, say so. Silence kills trust.
  • Answer phone/SMS the same hour on active freight.
  • No surprise extra fees. Weekly Stripe is the only operations bill from us.
The promise you are allowed to make

A named manager. Load finding and booking you approve. Paperwork follow-up. TMS. You keep broker pay. You can cancel before the first charge.

The promise you are not allowed to make

A dollar-per-mile number. “Always loaded.” “We pay you.” “We are your broker.” “No deadhead ever.”

9. Owner setup — Resend email + Twilio SMS

Sales does not put API keys in. The owner does this once in Resend, Twilio, and Vercel. Then the CRM buttons work.

Is Resend two-way?

Resend sends. Gmail (or Microsoft) receives. That is the correct professional setup. You can also turn on Resend inbound so replies appear inside our Inbox page. You do not need a second email vendor for sending. You do want Google Workspace (or similar) so the team can reply like a normal company.

  1. Resend.com → API Keys → copy key into Vercel: RESEND_API_KEY
  2. Resend → Domains → add shippingwish.com → add DNS SPF + DKIM. Wait until it says Verified.
  3. Add DMARC TXT: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:operations@shippingwish.com
  4. Vercel: MAIL_FROM=Shipping Wish LLC <operations@shippingwish.com> and MAIL_REPLY_TO=operations@shippingwish.com
  5. Keep MX records on Google Workspace so operations@ is a real inbox. Do not move MX to Resend.
  6. Optional two-way into CRM: Resend webhook email.received → https://www.shippingwish.com/api/email/inbound?secret=YOUR_SECRET and set RESEND_INBOUND_SECRET.

Spam / Promotions: nobody can promise Gmail Primary forever. Authenticated domain + real Reply-To + letter-style service emails + people actually replying is how you stay out of spam. Blast 500 cold emails in an hour and you will land in Promotions or the junk folder. Trial and load emails are service messages — those belong in Primary.

Twilio

  1. Twilio → Account SID + Auth Token → Vercel TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID / TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN / TWILIO_FROM_NUMBER
  2. Register A2P 10DLC (Trust Hub) or US carriers will filter you.
  3. Number webhook “A message comes in”: https://www.shippingwish.com/api/voip/twilio-inbound
  4. STOP / HELP is automatic. Do not remove that footer.

10. Workshop drill (30 minutes)

  1. Pair up. Person A is the tired owner-operator. Person B uses the 20-second open. Switch.
  2. Person A says “I got burned by a dispatcher.” Person B uses the burned script. No arguing.
  3. Open CRM on the projector. Import or add a fake lead. Click Email. Show Inbox.
  4. Show /pricing on the phone. Walk a test checkout in Stripe test mode if the owner allows it.
  5. Quiz: “Stripe checkout finished — where do you look?” Answer: CRM → Website Stripe checkouts / Active. Call in 15 minutes.
