Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Partnering

Got questions? We believe in 100% transparency. Here are honest, direct answers to what truckers ask us most.

Is there really no forced dispatch? What if I decline a load? +
Yes, strictly 100% zero forced dispatch. You are the owner-operator and you make the final call on every run. If you don't like a load's rate, appointment time, or destination state, you tell your dispatcher to pass and we continue hunting until you are completely satisfied.
How does payment work? Do you touch my freight money? +
We NEVER touch your money. Rate confirmations are issued directly in your company name. The broker pays 100% of your freight gross directly to your business bank account or factoring company (Apex, RTS, Triumph, etc.). Our weekly dispatch fee is billed separately via Stripe.
What documents do I need to get started today? +
Getting set up takes only 2 minutes online. You just need: (1) Your active MC Authority Certificate, (2) Certificate of Insurance (COI) with $1M Auto Liability & $100k Cargo, (3) Signed W-9 Form, and (4) Notice of Assignment (NOA) from your factoring company if you factor.
Do you work with new MC authorities under 90 days? +
Yes! We have extensive experience helping new motor carriers get rolling safely. We maintain strong relationships with a wide network of brokers and direct shippers that work with new authorities, helping you build a spotless credit and safety profile from month one.
How do you handle detention, layover, and TONU? +
We fight aggressively for every dollar you are owed. We require drivers to notify us as soon as they arrive at shippers/receivers. If you are held past the 2-hour standard window, we immediately submit signed in/out stamps and bill detention ($50–$75/hr). If a load is canceled after you are dispatched, we collect your Truck Order Not Used (TONU) fee.
Are there any long-term contracts? Can I cancel anytime? +
There are NO long-term contracts. You can cancel your dispatch subscription at any time with zero penalties. We believe in earning your business every single week through high-paying loads and exceptional service.
