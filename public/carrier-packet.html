6-Point Required Documents Checklist

Every motor carrier must provide these standard FMCSA compliance items before the first load is booked:

1

FMCSA Operating Authority

Copy of your active MC, FF, or MX Operating Authority Certificate issued by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA).

2

Certificate of Insurance (COI)

Must reflect minimum $1,000,000 Auto Liability and $100,000 Cargo Liability. Please list Shipping Wish LLC as Certificate Holder.

3

Signed Form W-9

Current year W-9 form signed and dated with your Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Social Security Number.

4

Factoring Setup / NOA

Notice of Assignment (NOA) from your factoring company for direct broker billing. If not factoring, provide a voided check for direct ACH.

5

Carrier Equipment Profile

Fill out Page 2 of the Onboarding Packet detailing equipment specs (53' Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed), trailer specs, max payload, and preferred lanes.

6

Limited Power of Attorney

Sign Page 3 of the packet authorizing Shipping Wish LLC to contact brokers, negotiate rates, and sign load Rate Confirmations strictly on your behalf.
