Platform Features

Not just a load board.
A complete freight intelligence platform.

LoadsNexus combines AI-powered matching, real-time market data, and enterprise-grade protection in one integrated platform.

🤖

AI Load Matching

Our AI engine analyzes your historical lanes, preferred equipment, rate floors, and deadhead tolerance to match you with the highest-value loads — before other carriers even see them.

📊

Real Broker Credit Scores

Every broker on the network shows a real Days-To-Pay score and credit rating pulled from live payment data. Know who pays fast and who doesn't — before you book.

🛡️

Anti-Double-Brokering Guard

Our Security Guard system detects and blocks double-brokering attempts in real time. Every load is verified. Carrier authority is cross-checked on every booking.

⚡

Live Market Rate Intelligence

See live spot rates, DAT/Truckstop market benchmarks, and lane-specific trends. Know exactly what your load is worth before you negotiate — and negotiate from strength.

Carrier & Broker Mobile Apps

Full TMS Integration
