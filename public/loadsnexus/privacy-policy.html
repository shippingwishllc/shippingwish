LoadsNexus™ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 • Last Revised: September 2026

Shipping Wish LLC doing business as LoadsNexus™ ("LoadsNexus," "Company," "we," "us," or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of motor carriers, owner-operators, freight brokers, shippers, and visitors who access our website at loadsnexus.com, mobile applications, and software platforms (collectively, the "Services").

Privacy Commitment: We never sell your personal information, contact lists, or mobile numbers to third-party data aggregators. Payment processing is handled exclusively through Stripe's certified Level-1 PCI-DSS infrastructure.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when using our platform:

  • Account Registration & Contact Info: Full name, business email address, phone number, physical address, company name, FMCSA Motor Carrier (MC) number, and USDOT number.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Credit card, debit card, and ACH billing details are collected directly by our payment processor, Stripe, Inc. LoadsNexus does not store or process raw credit card numbers or security CVV codes on our servers.
  • Freight & Capacity Postings: Origin, destination, equipment type (Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, etc.), load rates, pickup/delivery dates, commodity details, and weight specifications.
  • Broker Days-To-Pay (DTP) & Surety Bond Telemetry: Publicly available FMCSA licensing records, BMC-84 surety bond filings, and aggregated payment performance metrics.
  • Telematics & GPS Location Data: If you use our driver mobile app for active load tracking or automated lane matching, GPS coordinates are collected only with your explicit permission while the app is active.
  • Technical & Log Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and system access logs utilized by our Anti-Double-Brokering Guard to prevent unauthorized account access and freight fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for specific, legitimate operational purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the LoadsNexus spot freight exchange and capacity marketplace.
  • To match carriers with high-paying broker freight based on equipment specifications, routes, and deadhead optimization.
  • To generate official digital Rate Confirmation PDFs, carrier onboarding packets, and freight invoices.
  • To process monthly subscription fees ($19/mo Carrier Solo Pass, $39/mo Team Pass, $69/mo Fleet Pass) through Stripe.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent activity, double-brokering, carrier impersonation, and unauthorized access via our security guard.
  • To provide 24/7 customer support, respond to help tickets, and send essential transactional updates.

3. Third-Party Payment Processing (Stripe, Inc.)

Payment processing services for LoadsNexus are provided by Stripe, Inc. ("Stripe"). When subscribing to paid services, your payment information is transmitted directly to Stripe over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.

Stripe’s use of your personal information is governed by their Privacy Policy, which can be viewed at https://stripe.com/privacy. Stripe adheres to the standards set by PCI-DSS as managed by the PCI Security Standards Council.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Between Carriers & Brokers: When a carrier books a load, relevant business contact details, MC numbers, and equipment details are shared with the verified broker to issue the Rate Confirmation.
  • Payment & Financial Partners: Information required to complete payments, prevent chargeback disputes, and manage recurring subscriptions with Stripe.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by federal, state, or DOT/FMCSA authorities, or to comply with a valid subpoena or court order.
  • Anti-Fraud Protection: In the event of criminal freight double-brokering or cargo theft, audit logs may be provided to the FMCSA National Consumer Complaint Database and law enforcement agencies.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

LoadsNexus uses essential cookies and local storage tokens to keep you logged in to your load board workspace and retain your search filters. We do not use third-party tracking cookies to serve cross-site behavioral advertisements.

6. 10DLC SMS & Mobile Communications Compliance

By providing your mobile telephone number during signup or carrier onboarding, you consent to receive operational text messages (SMS) from LoadsNexus and Shipping Wish LLC:

  • Message Scope: Rate confirmation alerts, urgent load updates, subscription billing receipts, and dispatcher responses.
  • Sending Phone Numbers: SMS messages are dispatched from registered carrier lines including +1 (609) 469-6004 and +1 (800) 580-3101.
  • Opt-Out: You may reply STOP at any time to immediately cancel text message notifications. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • No Mobile Number Sale: We will never sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

7. Data Security & Retention

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational security controls, including 256-bit AES encryption at rest, TLS encryption in transit, strict database role-based access control (RBAC), and continuous threat monitoring. We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active or as required by commercial accounting and DOT record-keeping regulations.

8. Your Rights & Choices (CCPA / CPRA / GDPR)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate records, or request complete deletion of your profile. To exercise these rights, please email us at support@loadsnexus.com.

9. Children’s Privacy

LoadsNexus is an enterprise B2B freight software platform intended solely for authorized commercial entities and individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements to our platform, changes in statutory requirements, or updates from our financial partners. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Revised" date.
