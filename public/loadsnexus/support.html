Frequently Asked Support Questions

❓ How do I cancel my LoadsNexus monthly Carrier Pass?
You can cancel your subscription at any time without calling. Simply log in to your account, navigate to Billing Settings, and select "Cancel Subscription". You can also email us at support@loadsnexus.com or use the ticket form above. Your access will remain active until the end of your prepaid period with no further charges.
❓ How does payment processing work on LoadsNexus?
All subscription and platform transactions on LoadsNexus are processed through Stripe, Inc., a PCI Service Provider Level 1 certified gateway. We never store or see your raw credit card numbers or banking secrets on our servers.
❓ How can I report a fraudulent load or double-broker?
LoadsNexus operates a zero-tolerance Anti-Double-Brokering Guard. If you encounter any unauthorized re-brokered freight, impersonated broker MC, or fraudulent paperwork, select "Report Double-Brokering" in the ticket form above or call our security desk at +1 (800) 580-3101. We immediately investigate, freeze bad actors, and notify FMCSA.
❓ How do I dispute or update a Days-To-Pay (DTP) score?
Brokers who wish to provide updated payment remittance documentation, surety bond endorsements, or credit references can send verified records to support@loadsnexus.com. Our compliance team audits remittance logs within 24 business hours.
