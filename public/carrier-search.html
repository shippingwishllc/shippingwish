Questions

FMCSA carrier lookup FAQ

Is this the same as FMCSA SAFER?

This tool queries the same underlying Company Census that powers many government carrier profiles. For safety ratings and full authority history, also open the carrier on FMCSA SAFER.

Can I search by phone number or email?

Yes. Choose Phone or Email in the dropdown, or use Auto when the query looks like a phone or email. Not every carrier has both fields on file.

Is this legal to use on a public website?

Yes. The U.S. DOT publishes the Company Census as open data for public use. We do not resell the data — this is a free lookup with source attribution and disclaimers.
