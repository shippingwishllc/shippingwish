How Motive-style ELD works here

Connect Motive, Samsara, or Geotab. Do not clone hardware.

Motive is a registered ELD company. Shipping Wish is dispatch. The legal path is the same as other TMS tools: the fleet admin signs in to their own ELD account, grants consent, and we read GPS + HOS from the official API. We do not scrape ELD portals, do not sell a competing device, and do not invent truck locations.
