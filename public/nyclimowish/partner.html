Partner Requirements

We maintain the highest standards of safety, discretion, and luxury for our clientele.

👤 For Chauffeurs:

  • Valid Driver’s License along with active commercial auto insurance.
  • Commercial Driving License / TLC License (required for NYC metropolitan area).
  • Clean, late-model luxury vehicle (e.g., Business Sedan, Luxury SUV, Premium SUV) in pristine condition — 100% smoke-free.
  • Clean driving record and commitment to punctual, professional executive service.

🏢 For Affiliate Fleet Companies:

  • Valid corporate company registration & TLC / USDOT / MC Operating Authority.
  • Fleet roster with full vehicle specifications (Make, Model, Year, Color, Capacity).
  • Commercial liability insurance certificate ($1.5M+ combined single limit coverage).
  • Professional background-checked chauffeurs compliant with local regulations.
