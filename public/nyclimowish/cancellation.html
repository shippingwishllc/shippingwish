Cancellation Policy

We understand plans change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both passengers and chauffeurs.

Standard Cancellations

More than 24 hours before pickup: Full refund minus processing fees.
12–24 hours before pickup: 50% charge.
Less than 12 hours before pickup: Full fare charged.

No-Shows

If the passenger is not at the pickup location within the complimentary wait period and cannot be reached, the booking is considered a no-show and the full fare applies.

Flight Delays

For airport pickups, we monitor flights at no extra charge. Significant delays may require rebooking subject to vehicle availability.

How to Cancel

Cancel via your confirmation email link, the passenger app at /passenger, or call (917) 737-0021.
