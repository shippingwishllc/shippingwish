How It Works

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

We charge a percentage (typically 5–10%) of the gross rate on every load we book for you. We send you a weekly invoice for our commission. You receive full payment from the broker or factoring company directly. No other fees.
We primarily dispatch Dry Van, Flatbed, Reefer, and Step Deck equipment. If you have specialized equipment, contact us and we will discuss your specific needs.
No long-term contracts. Our dispatcher–carrier agreement is straightforward and can be terminated with proper notice. We earn your continued business by delivering results.
In most cases, we can complete onboarding and start booking loads within 24–48 hours. We need your carrier packet, authority, insurance, and a signed agreement to begin.
Your carrier portal includes real-time load status, driver management, truck and trailer records, document storage, invoice history, performance analytics, and broker directory access.
We do not provide factoring directly — we assist you in setting up factoring with established providers like RTS Financial, Apex Capital, and others. The factoring relationship is between you and the provider.
Your dedicated dispatcher is your first point of contact. Our team is available 24/7 for active load issues — breakdowns, appointment delays, detention, or broker communication.
We send a professional weekly invoice for our commission amount via email. Payment is accepted through Stripe (bank transfer / ACH). We do not hold your freight revenue.
