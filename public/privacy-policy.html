Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Shipping Wish LLC ("Shipping Wish," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through shippingwish.com and our operations services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name, company name, phone number, and email address submitted through our forms or by phone.
  • Trucking details such as equipment type, number of trucks, preferred lanes, and authority status.
  • Account information, including a hashed password, when you create a login.
  • Messages or documents you send us in connection with operations services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to service requests and assign an operations manager.
  • To find and book freight and handle related paperwork.
  • To send confirmation emails, billing notices, and service updates.
  • To improve our website and services.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with brokers, factoring companies, or carriers as needed to book and complete a load, and with service providers who help us operate the site, under confidentiality obligations.

4. Cookies & Login Sessions

When you sign in, we use a secure, httpOnly cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use this cookie for advertising or tracking across other websites.

5. Data Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Choices

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request deletion by emailing info@shippingwish.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business use by adults in the trucking industry and are not directed to individuals under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
