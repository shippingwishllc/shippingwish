Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 | Shipping Wish LLC & Affiliated Brands

1. Introduction & Scope

Shipping Wish LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates freight dispatch, logistics management, telematics coordination, and affiliated platforms including LoadsNexus™, NYC Limo Wish, and BuyWish Online. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and business data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide dispatch, transportation, load booking, and platform services:

  • Carrier & Driver Details: USDOT/MC numbers, certificates of insurance (COI), W-9 forms, Rate Confirmations, Bills of Lading (BOL).
  • Account Information: Name, business email, contact phone number, billing address.
  • Social Media & API Integrations: Authorized API tokens (Meta, Facebook Pages, Instagram, LinkedIn) used strictly for publishing business updates and customer engagement on behalf of authorized brands.

3. How We Use Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To execute freight dispatch, negotiate carrier rates, and audit RateCons.
  • To provide customer support and real-time shipment/trip notifications via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.
  • To publish approved social media marketing content and industry updates across connected company channels.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers.

4. Data Security & Retention

All data and API tokens are encrypted in transit via SSL/TLS and stored securely behind protected databases with strict role-based access control. Tokens and sensitive credentials can be revoked at any time by company administrators.
