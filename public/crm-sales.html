Call track — never lead with “dispatch”

Carriers hang up on “dispatch companies.” Open as Shipping Wish LLC offering a Dedicated Fleet Operations Manager for their trucks. Weekly retainer. They keep broker pay.

  1. “Hi, this is ___ with Shipping Wish LLC. I’m not calling to dump loads. We place a Dedicated Operations Manager with small fleets. Do you already have that person in-house?”
  2. If they pause: “Small weekly Stripe invoice. You collect 100% of freight from the broker. We find and book. Useful, or are you covered?”
  3. If they say they got burned: “Understood. This is a named manager, not a call center. I can email the one-pager — no pressure.”
