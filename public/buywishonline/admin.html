Products

Sync keeps a product only when Zendrop quotes shipping to the USA, Canada, and the United Kingdom, and the fastest USA lane is 14 days or fewer. Run it again until the batch is finished. Paid orders are sent to Zendrop only when Zendrop publishes an order-creation tool for this token. Otherwise the order stays here with the customer’s address so it can be placed in the Zendrop dashboard and linked.

Catalog

Create a category, add a product by its numeric supplier ID, move a product into another category, or remove it from the shop. Built-in categories stay in place. A removed product stays hidden until you add that same ID again.

Deals and margin

If the supplier price is only what you pay, the shop adds your margin before the customer sees it. If the supplier already lists a higher selling price, that price stays. A two-product offer must stay above supplier cost and below the two selling prices added together.
