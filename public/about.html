We are an independent LLC based in Delaware, combining modern software with dedicated support to keep fleets moving efficiently across 48 states.

Our Mission & Vision

At Shipping Wish, we envision a trucking industry where owner-operators and small fleets have access to the same enterprise-grade technology and dedicated support as the largest carriers.

Our mission is to maximize your revenue while minimizing your administrative burden, acting as an extension of your team rather than just a service provider.

How We're Different

We are built differently to serve you better.

Not a Broker

We don't buy or sell freight. We work strictly on your behalf to negotiate the best possible rates from brokers and shippers.

Not a Call Center

You get a dedicated dispatcher who knows your lanes, preferences, and operational needs, not a random voice on the phone.

Technology-First

Powered by a real Transportation Management System (TMS), streamlining document handling, invoicing, and ELD compliance.

Our Core Values

Transparency

No hidden fees. You see exactly what the broker pays.

Performance

Driven by data to maximize your gross revenue per week.

Availability

24/7 support when you are on the road. We don't sleep until you do.

Integrity

Doing the right thing, even when no one is watching.

Verified & Compliant

We operate as a fully registered LLC in the State of Delaware. We utilize Stripe for secure, professional invoicing and strictly adhere to FMCSA guidelines by acting solely as a dispatch service, not a freight broker.
