Three ways a load reaches the board

  1. A broker posts it on LoadsNexus. That path is already live.
  2. You paste a licensed load-board API here. The desk calls that https JSON URL with your key. It does not open websites or copy another board’s pages.
  3. When a load is covered, expired, or missing from the latest API reply, it leaves the public board.

The board stays empty until a broker posts or an API returns real loads. Start sends a good-morning text asking for the empty ZIP. A broker is only contacted after the driver picks that exact load. Home-time days are a skip rule, not forced dispatch.

What the AI dispatcher does with a driver's text

  1. Reads texts like “75201 to Atlanta”, “empty in Dallas TX want GA or FL reefer”, “MORE”, “off today”, “EMPTY SOON”, or “HOME FRI SAT”.
  2. Finds open loads within the carrier's empty-mile limit, with matching equipment and destination, above their minimum $/mile. It ranks them by all-in $/mile, counting empty miles too. Home days (for example FRI,SAT) skip a pickup on those days unless the load is heading to the home state, and skip runs that would miss the next home evening.
  3. Texts the best three. The driver replies 1, 2 or 3. About two to three hours before a booked drop, it texts the next three from that empty ZIP if the carrier agreed to texts.
  4. Emails the broker a booking request with the carrier's MC or DOT and tells the driver not to roll yet.
  5. If the broker replies “covered”, the driver gets the next best loads. If the broker confirms, you check the rate confirmation and press Booked. Then the driver gets a BOOKED text.

Rates shown are the broker's posted rate. Miles marked ~ are estimated from ZIP and city locations. After 12 texts to one carrier in a day, the AI hands off to a person. STOP pauses the carrier.

FMCSA email outreach

AI pulls active carriers (1–20 trucks) and brokers with an email on file from the public FMCSA registry. Carriers get up to four emails over about three weeks, from Shipping Wish and LoadsNexus. Brokers get up to three emails from LoadsNexus. Emails go out Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. in each company’s own time zone.

It starts small and raises the daily limit only while bounces and unsubscribes stay low. It slows down when they rise and pauses itself if they cross the safe line. A reply, an unsubscribe, a bounce, or a signup stops the emails to that company. Cold contacts never get texts or AI calls.
