AI Dispatch

Start work for each carrier you add. Loads come from brokers or a licensed JSON API.

Three ways a load reaches the board

  1. A broker posts it on LoadsNexus. That path is already live.
  2. You paste a licensed load-board API here. The desk calls that https JSON URL with your key. It does not open websites or copy another board’s pages.
  3. When a load is covered, expired, or missing from the latest API reply, it leaves the public board.

The board stays empty until a broker posts or an API returns real loads. Start sends a good-morning text asking for the empty ZIP. A broker is only contacted after the driver picks that exact load.

What the AI dispatcher does with a driver's text

  1. Reads texts like “75201 to Atlanta”, “empty in Dallas TX want GA or FL reefer”, “MORE”, “RELOAD”, “ARRIVED SHIPPER”, “LOADED”, “WAITING”, or “off today”.
  2. Finds open loads within the carrier's empty-mile limit, with matching equipment and destination, above their minimum $/mile. It ranks them by all-in $/mile, counting empty miles too.
  3. Texts the best three. The driver replies 1, 2 or 3.
  4. When a broker posts a load that fits an active carrier who already consented to texts, the desk texts that load during 9am–5pm in their timezone (max 12 outbound texts per carrier per day). It does not interrupt a driver who already has an open list or a pending booking.
  5. Emails the broker a booking request with the carrier's MC or DOT and tells the driver not to roll yet.
  6. If the broker replies “covered”, the driver gets the next best loads. If the broker confirms, you check the rate confirmation and press Booked. Then the driver gets a BOOKED text and a reload hint from the delivery city. On the road they text ARRIVED SHIPPER, LOADED, WAITING, ARRIVED RECEIVER, or DELIVERED, and they send a photo of the POD. Check-calls go out during 9am–5pm if they have not written in. Detention is clocked in minutes; no dollar amount is billed unless you set a rate. They reply RELOAD when empty.

Rates shown are the broker's posted rate. Miles marked ~ are estimated from ZIP and city locations. After 12 texts to one carrier in a day, the AI hands off to a person. STOP pauses the carrier.

FMCSA email outreach

AI pulls active carriers (1–20 trucks) and brokers with an email on file from the public FMCSA registry. Carriers get up to four emails over about three weeks, from Shipping Wish and LoadsNexus. Brokers get up to three emails from LoadsNexus. Emails go out Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. in each company’s own time zone.

It starts small and raises the daily limit only while bounces and unsubscribes stay low. It slows down when they rise and pauses itself if they cross the safe line. A reply, an unsubscribe, a bounce, or a signup stops the emails to that company. Cold contacts never get texts or AI calls.
